Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 September 2026

1. Overview

n8n YouTube Uploader ("the Application") is a private automation tool operated by its owner to upload and schedule video content on the owner's own YouTube channel. The Application is not offered as a public service.

2. Information We Access

When you authorize the Application with your Google Account, it requests access to YouTube data in order to:

  • Upload video files to your YouTube channel
  • Set video metadata (title, description, tags, category, privacy status, scheduled publish time)
  • Upload video thumbnails
  • Read and modify your playlists in order to add uploaded videos

The Application does not access your Gmail, Google Drive, contacts, calendar, or any other Google service.

3. How Information Is Used

Data accessed through the YouTube Data API is used solely to perform the upload and scheduling actions described above. It is never used for advertising, profiling, analytics, or resale.

4. Storage

The Application runs locally on the owner's own computer. OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored locally by the n8n automation platform on that machine and are not transmitted to any third-party server other than Google's own APIs. No user data is stored on any hosted database or cloud service operated by us.

5. Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share any data obtained through the Google APIs with any third party.

6. Google API Services User Data Policy

The Application's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

7. Revoking Access

You may revoke the Application's access to your Google Account at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Revoking access immediately stops all further API activity.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Locally stored tokens are deleted when the corresponding credential is removed from the local n8n instance or when access is revoked through the Google Account permissions page.

9. Children

The Application is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

10. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The current version is always available at this page.
